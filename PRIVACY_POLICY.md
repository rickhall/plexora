# Privacy Policy for Plexora

Last Updated: October 2023

Plexora is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Plexora application on Android Automotive OS.

## 1. Information We Collect

Plexora is designed to be a private and local-first application. 

*   **Plex Authentication Data**: When you link your Plex account, we use the standard Plex.tv PIN linking process. We store an authentication token and your server's connection URL locally on your device.
*   **Media Metadata**: We fetch and cache metadata (artist names, album titles, and artwork) from your own Plex Media Server to provide the browsing experience.
*   **No Personal Data Collection**: Plexora does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal information (such as your name, email address, or location) to any third-party servers, including our own.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Plexora interacts with the following third-party services:

*   **Plex.tv**: Used solely for the initial authentication and to discover your available Plex Media Servers.
*   **Your Plex Media Server**: Plexora connects directly to your server to stream your music library.

Please refer to the [Plex Privacy Policy](https://www.plex.tv/about/privacy-legal/) for information on how they handle your data.

## 3. Data Storage

All configuration data, including your Plex token and server preferences, is stored securely and locally on your vehicle's head unit using Android's standard encrypted storage. This data is cleared if you choose to "Sign Out" or uninstall the application.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us via our GitHub repository.
